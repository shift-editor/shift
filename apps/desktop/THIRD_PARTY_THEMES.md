## Bundled base16 themes

The theme files in `themes/` are converted from schemes in the Tinted Theming project. Each file names its scheme and author; a few add `[colors]` overrides so handles and text stay readable in Shift.

- Source: https://github.com/tinted-theming/schemes
- Copyright © 2022 Tinted Theming
- License: MIT
